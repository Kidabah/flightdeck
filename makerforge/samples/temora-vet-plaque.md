# Temora Vet Clinic plaque (MakerDeck)

## Load in MakerDeck
1. Hard refresh MakerDeck (**b515+**)
2. Design → Signs → **Temora Vet plaque**
3. Wait for text + Celtic frame SVG to rebuild
4. Export → Download **3MF**
5. Open in **Orca** (recommended for colours)

## Spec (v1)
- Size: **180 × 120 × 4 mm**, rounded, keyhole mounts, raised border
- Text (exact):
  - TEMORA / VET CLINIC
  - "WE LOVE YOUR PETS / AS MUCH AS YOU"
  - ABBIE, SIOBHAN, SHARLET, / KAYT, STEPH, LUCY
- Art: [`temora-vet-celtic-frame.svg`](temora-vet-celtic-frame.svg) embossed frame (hollow centre)
- Colours: silver plate `#C5CAD3`, black text/knot

## Files
- `temora-vet-celtic-frame.svg` — Celtic-style border for SVG emboss
- Preset: `TEMORA_VET_SIGN_PRESET` in `js/geometry.js`
