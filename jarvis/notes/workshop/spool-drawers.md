# Spool storage drawers

Flightdeck uses six physical drawers for filament homes:

- Drawers **D1–D6**
- Three rows per drawer **R1–R3**
- Nine positions per row
- Global spool positions **#1–#162**

A spool's **home** is the numbered drawer slot matching its spool number (spool #42 → `D? R? #42`). Home stays reserved while the spool is loaded in a printer or visiting the SUNLU dryer.

**Fast assign** on the Spools page places leftover legacy-shelf spools into empty drawer homes. Undo is available for the last Fast Assign batch.

Labels print on the Brother QL-700 (DK-22212 62 mm continuous) from Flightdeck.
