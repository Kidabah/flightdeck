# Amy sleep + idle

After ~3 minutes quiet she may whistle and nudge you. After ~6 minutes she enters **sleep** — circle goes dark.

Wake her with **wake up Amy**, **hey Amy**, click her face, or MIC/ASK.

Ask her to **whistle** or **sing** anytime (voice or type) and she does the little tone on demand.

Mic can stay on while she sleeps so the wake phrase works.
